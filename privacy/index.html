Plain-language policy · 28 August 2026

Privacy, kept in your kitchen

Your recipes, weekly plans, school meals, tags, and outcomes are stored in this browser’s IndexedDB. We do not receive them.

What stays on your device

All planning data stays locally unless you choose to export a JSON or CSV file. Clearing this site’s browser storage removes it. Use the backup export before clearing or changing devices.

License verification

If you buy or restore the one-time unlock, the license token and a daily verification result are stored in localStorage. Verification sends only that token to the Sociobot billing API. Sociobot/Dodo is the merchant of record and handles checkout details; this app never sees card data.

Network and analytics

The planner works offline after its first load. It contains no advertising trackers, third-party fonts, or behavioral analytics. The hosting platform may keep short-lived security and aggregate request logs.

Your control

Use “Export backup” to take your data with you and “Erase local data” to remove planning data from this browser. Questions can be sent to privacy@sociobot.in.
